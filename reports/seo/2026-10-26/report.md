- URL Inspection reports Google's latest known state, not a guaranteed live crawl or an indexing promise.
- Position is an impression-weighted average, not a fixed live rank.

- Low-volume queries may be omitted by Google for privacy.
- Every recommendation requires human review. Immigration claims must remain grounded in authoritative sources.
- Community stories are outside the automatic edit scope.
