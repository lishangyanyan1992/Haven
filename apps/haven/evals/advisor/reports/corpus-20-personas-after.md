## clock-paid-through

If your cessation date was May 30, 2026, the 60 days would run through July 29, 2026; if your cessation date was July 4, 2026, the 60 days would run through September 2, 2026. Confirm the employer’s official termination date/reporting and the exact deadline with counsel immediately.

Required legal points and safety reminders
- The grace period is up to 60 days or until the I‑94/petition validity ends, whichever is shorter (8 C.F.R. § 214.1). DHS can shorten or eliminate the 60 days as a matter of discretion.  
- Do not work without authorization. Work is not authorized during the grace period unless you have separate work authorization or a qualifying properly filed portability petition.  
- LCA preparation alone does not preserve status. An LCA or petition-in‑preparation is not the same as a properly filed, nonfrivolous H‑1B petition.  
- Portability depends on a new employer properly filing a nonfrivolous H‑1B petition while you remain in an authorized period (8 C.F.R. § 214.2).

Concrete options to raise with counsel (do not treat as legal advice)

- Premium processing / employer escalation: ask the prospective employer about premium processing or expedited handling (timing and fee details vary).  
- Immediate counsel review: have an immigration attorney confirm the employer’s termination date, your exact deadline, and the best filing strategy (DHS discretion means you should not assume the full 60 days).

Urgent filing point
